🌳 Unit 1 Project: Place Value Adventure

Students will apply their understanding of place value to create a "Marketplace of Madinah" project. They will simulate fair trade, use place value for large numbers, and connect math to values of justice (عدل) and honesty (أمانة).

📌 Project Steps

  1. Form groups and assign roles (Leader, Recorder, Reflector, Worker).
  2. Create trade scenarios with prices up to one million.
  3. Represent numbers in standard, word, and expanded forms.
  4. Show fairness by checking place values and ensuring accurate transactions.
  5. Reflect daily on honesty and fairness in group journals.

📊 Rubric

  • Math Accuracy (40%)
  • Creativity & Presentation (20%)
  • Collaboration & Roles (20%)
  • Akhlāq Values (20%)
