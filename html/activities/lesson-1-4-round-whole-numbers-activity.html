Stage 5 STEAM Production Builds
Choose 1 of 3 student production artifacts to build and defend against the Proof Critic.
map
A halfway map: draw the line between two rounded values, mark the halfway point, and place a real number on the right side of it with the rounding written beside it.
ask which side of halfway a number sits on → plan the line and the halfway mark → build the map → test it with a number exactly on halfway → share it with the council
presentation
A 60-second 'why this precision is enough' talk: round the district populations to the requested place, defend why that precision is enough for the council's decision, and show the halfway line behind the call.
ask how precise the estimate must be → plan the argument → round to the requested place → test it against the halfway line → share it at the council table
mini-project AI in the loop
A one-page estimation report on a real number: research one real budget or population figure with the AI, verify it, round it the lesson way, and write the report. The AI challenges your rounding once and you defend it.
ask what the council actually needs → plan the report → research a real figure with the AI (verify it) → round and explain → test against the halfway line → share it on the wall
AI in the Loop (Critic / Defense Rule)
The AI examines, challenges, and audits candidate calculations. Students verify and build every piece themselves — AI is a critic, never an author.
