Mission 1: Multiples Hunt
Task: Find the first 5 multiples of 6
💡 Example: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30
⚡ Challenge: Multiples of 9 up to 90
🚫 Watch out: Don't mix multiples with factors
🔄 Remember: Multiples = skip counting forward
🎯
Mission 2: Multiple or Not?
Task: Is 48 a multiple of 8?
💡 Example: Yes! 8×6=48
⚡ Challenge: Test if 50 is a multiple of 8
🌱 Akhlāq: إتقان → check carefully
🎯
Mission 3: Shape Pattern Builder
Task: Extend 🔺🔵🔺🔵...
⚡ Challenge: Create your own pattern
🚫 Watch out: Random shapes break patterns
🔄 Remember: Identify the repeating "unit"
🎯
Mission 4: Growing Numbers
Task: Continue 3, 6, 9, 12...
⚡ Challenge: Rule = "Add 3 each time"
🚫 Watch out: Random additions
🔄 Remember: Keep rule consistent
🎯
Mission 5: Real-Life Pattern
Task: Bus every 5 minutes → list first 30 mins
⚡ Challenge: How many stops in 1 hour?
💡 Connection: Multiples = real-life schedules
📝 Exit Ticket Questions
Q1: Is 63 a multiple of 9? Explain your thinking.
Q2: Extend this pattern: 2, 4, 8, 16, __, __
