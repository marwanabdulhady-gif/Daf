🎯 Mission 1: Understanding the Difference

Scenario: Ali has 4 stickers. How many does Sara have?

📈 Additive: "Sara has 3 MORE"

Sara = Ali + 3

Sara = 4 + 3 = 7 stickers

✖️ Multiplicative: "Sara has 3 TIMES AS MANY"

Sara = Ali × 3

Sara = 4 × 3 = 12 stickers

Your Turn: Omar has 6 books. Layla has 4 times as many. How many books does Layla have?

✍️ Mission 2: Writing Perfect Equations

Problem: Lina has 5 times as many pencils as Noor. Noor has 6 pencils. How many pencils does Lina have?

Step-by-Step:

1. Identify what we know: Noor = 6 pencils

2. Identify the relationship: Lina = 5 × Noor's amount

3. Write the equation: 5 × 6 = 30

4. Answer: Lina has 30 pencils

📏 Mission 3: Real-World Scaling

Scenario: A toy car is 12 cm long. The real car is 40 times longer. How long is the real car?

🚗 Scaling Formula:

Real Size = Toy Size × Scale Factor

Real Car = 12 cm × 40 = 480 cm = 4.8 meters

أمانة في القياس
Precision in measurements matters! Exaggerating or being careless with scales can mislead people.
