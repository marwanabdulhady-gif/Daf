🏆 Summit Challenge: Remainders

Solve real-world problems with remainders!

Problem 1: Packing Backpacks 🎒

You have 23 items to pack. Each backpack holds 4 items. How many full backpacks can you pack?

23 ÷ 4 = remainder

What should you do with the remainder?

Correct! You can pack 5 full backpacks and leave 3 items behind.

Problem 2: Hiring Guides 👥

29 explorers need guides. Each guide can help 8 people. How many guides do you need?

29 ÷ 8 = remainder

What should you do with the remainder?

Excellent! You need 4 guides total (3 full groups + 1 extra for the remaining 5 people).

Problem 3: Sharing Treasure 💰

You found 17 gold coins to share equally among 3 explorers. How much does each person get?

17 ÷ 3 = remainder

What should you do with the remainder?

Perfect! Each explorer gets 5 coins, with 2 coins left to share later or save.
