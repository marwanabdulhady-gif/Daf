Choose 1 of 3 student production artifacts to build and defend against the Proof Critic.
design
A roof area-model design: draw the roof as a rectangle, partition it the way the class chose, label every partial area, and total the panels — sized so a classmate could price the roof from your drawing alone.
ask how to split the roof → plan the partition → draw and label the model → test the total with a second split → share it at the gate
song
A partial-products chant for the roof: one verse per partial area, a chorus that adds them into the total — with a clap rhythm that carries the place-value parts.
ask what the roof's parts are → plan the verses → write the chant → test it on a classmate (they total the parts) → share it at the dry channel
map AI in the loop
A partition map from a real roof: research one real long roof or fence length with the AI, verify it, then draw the area model for the panel count and map every partial area to a part of the real measurement.
ask what real length to model → plan the map → research a real measurement with the AI (verify it) → test the model with a second split → share it on the wall
