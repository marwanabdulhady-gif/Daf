🎯 Array Architect Missions

🎯 Mission 1: Break It Apart

Task: Solve 7 × (3 + 2)

💡 Example: (7×3) + (7×2) = 21 + 14 = 35

⚡ Challenge: Try 9 × (4 + 6)

🚫 Misconception: Add first, then multiply

🔄 Reteach: Step-by-step decomposition

🎯 Mission 2: Array Builder

Task: Build array for 6 × 7 → (6×5)+(6×2)

⚡ Challenge: Break 8 × 9 in 2 ways

🌱 Akhlāq: إتقان – neat, careful work

🎯 Mission 3: Word Problem Split

Task: 9 rows × 12 plants per row

💡 Example: (9×10)+(9×2) = 90+18 = 108

⚡ Challenge: Try with 14 plants per row

🎯 Mission 4: Factor Flexibility

Task: 15 × 8 = (10×8)+(5×8)

⚡ Challenge: 27 × 4 = (20×4)+(7×4)

🚫 Misconception: Forgetting both parts

🔄 Reteach: Use arrows to connect

🎯 Mission 5: Real-Life Application

Task: 13 packs × 6 bottles each

💡 Solution: (10×6)+(3×6) = 60+18 = 78

⚡ Challenge: 18 packs × 7 bottles
