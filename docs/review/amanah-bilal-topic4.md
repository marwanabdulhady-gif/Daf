## Theme

Perseverance: continuing careful work when it is hard, and when others do not understand it.

## Source candidates for the reviewer's consideration

- The standard seerah lineage: Ibn Ishaq via Ibn Hisham's recension; Albert Guillaume's English
  translation — the early Muslims in Makkah under persecution, and the account of Bilal ibn Rabah
  (born in Makkah into slavery; a believer from the earliest community; later known as the first
  mu'adhdhin of the new faith).
- The reviewer may designate any other approved source or text; the mention of Bilal ibn Rabah
  should be as brief as the approved source allows.

## Chronology anchors (for the reviewer to confirm or correct)

- In the earliest years in Makkah, the new believers were few and the pressure on them was
  constant; the seerah literature records perseverance under that pressure.
- Bilal ibn Rabah, from Makkah, is recorded among the earliest believers; his story is one of
  faith held through hardship, before the Hijrah carried the community to Madinah.

## Proposed student-facing text (DRAFT — the reviewer's text ships)

> In the earliest years in Makkah, the new believers were few, and staying true was hard. The
> seerah records their perseverance under pressure. Among them was Bilal ibn Rabah, from Makkah,
> one of the earliest to believe — a man who held on when holding on cost him.
>
> **Values reflection — perseverance:** a jammed mechanism does not argue with the jam. It is
> worked through: check the cause, adjust, try again. The lift in this lesson moved the same way
> the early believers held: step by careful step, when the work resisted.
