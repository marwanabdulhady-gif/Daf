## Theme

Knowledge carried with responsibility: the command to read, and the trust that comes with being
able to read and record.

## Source candidates for the reviewer's consideration

- The Qur'an, sūrah al-ʿAlaq 96:1–5 ("Read in the name of your Lord…") — the opening of the
  revelation, the command to read.
- The standard seerah lineage: Ibn Ishaq via Ibn Hisham's recension; Albert Guillaume's English
  translation (the first revelation, cave of Hira, the account of the command to read).
- The reviewer may designate any other approved source or text.

## Chronology anchors (for the reviewer to confirm or correct)

- After years of known honesty in Makkah (see the Topic 1 Al-Amin window), the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
  received the first revelation in the cave of Hira, with the command to read — at the beginning of
  the mission, before any community existed.
- The command to read comes at the start of a life of teaching; knowledge is framed as something
  received and then carried, not owned.

## Proposed student-facing text (DRAFT — the reviewer's text ships)

> The first revelation began with a command: read. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was told to read — and
> to keep reading. Knowledge, in this story, is not a possession. It is a trust: what you are given
> to know, you carry carefully, and you use it to make records true.
>
> **Values reflection — the responsibility of knowledge:** a record you can read is a record you
> are responsible for. That is the same test the ledger in this lesson passed: the audit was
> earned with care, not speed.
