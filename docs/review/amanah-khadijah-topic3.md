## Theme

Truthful support and steadiness: backing a decision with honesty and staying with it when the
work is hard.

## Source candidates for the reviewer's consideration

- The standard seerah lineage: Ibn Ishaq via Ibn Hisham's recension; Albert Guillaume's English
  translation — Khadijah (r.a.) before and at the beginning of the mission: her standing in
  Makkah, her honest trade, her marriage to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, and her support at the
  beginning of the mission (her steadiness when he returned from the cave of Hira).
- The reviewer may designate any other approved source or text.

## Chronology anchors (for the reviewer to confirm or correct)

- Khadijah (r.a.) was known in Makkah for honest dealing; she employed the young Muhammad ﷺ, and
  on the strength of his honesty she proposed marriage — an account of trust earned through
  truthful work.
- At the beginning of the mission, her support and steadiness are recorded in the seerah
  literature; the window may name her role as support and steadiness, and nothing more.

## Proposed student-facing text (DRAFT — the reviewer's text ships)

> Before the mission began, Khadijah (r.a.) was known in Makkah for honest dealing. She trusted
> the young Muhammad ﷺ with her trade because his word was his bond. And at the beginning of the
> mission, her support was steady. She did not argue with the work — she backed it with her
> honesty and her calm.
>
> **Values reflection — truthful support:** a design stands when the people around it are honest
> about what is true and steady when it is hard. The grove in this lesson stands the same way:
> the rows were counted honestly, and the plan held because the numbers were true.
