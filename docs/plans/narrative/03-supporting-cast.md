# 03 · The supporting cast — fictional characters of the seventeen doors

**Status:** design + build spec (characters are now drawn, payload-carried and woven
into the built decks). Companion to `00-epic-architecture.md` (structure) and
`01-characters-and-arcs.md` (the two protagonists and the Muddler).
**Guardrails carried forward:** every character here is an **original fictional**
person of present-day Jeddah — modest contemporary clothing, flat vector, no
resemblance to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, his companions or any historical person.
No companion or historical name (Bilal, Khadijah, Banu Musa, al-Jazari, al-Idrisi,
al-Kharizmi…) is used as a fictional character name; documented history lives only
in the STEM windows' "documented account" treatment. Sacred content is never a
quest, a boss, a reward or a role-play.
**The class is boys only** (school policy, enforced by `check:story`): every
*student* character — the two protagonists, the rival Rashed and the whole class
ensemble (listed in the story map as `characters.ensemble`, all gender "boy") —
is a boy. The adult quest givers in `characters.supporting` are community adults,
not students, and are outside that rule. (Ensemble names that read as girls were
renamed to boys in 2026-08 across all 114 lessons: Aya→Hassan, Sara→Hani,
Huda→Samer, Rana→Karam, Layla→Layth, Ola→Numan, Reem→Anees, Noura→Joud,
Dina→Lamees, Maya→Mazin, Yara→Firas, Zahra→Marwan, Hala→Qais, Aisha→Nidal,

## 1 · Why a cast, and what each figure does to the story

The two protagonists carry the arc; the **cast carries the world**. Each figure is
borrowed as a *function*, not a copy:

| Function (the inspiration) | Our character | Job in the quest |
|---|---|---|
| The frame keeper / the teller (Nights: the storyteller who holds the tale; LOTR: Elrond who holds the council) | **Hafizah, the Lantern Keeper** | Gives the class the lantern and the damaged folios (door 1); receives the completed folio and the lantern's new keepers (door 17). The tale's bookends. |
| The traveling guide (LOTR: the guide who keeps the party on the road; Nights: the traveler who brings a riddle; HP: the mentor who arrives at the right moment) | **Ustadh Zafir, the traveling scholar** | Appears at act gates and mid-quest riddles; names the fog at the door-5 climax; warns the class about the Muddler before door 13. |
| The gentle rival (HP: the rival school, made constructive) | **Rashed, the Rival's Report** | Authors the "polished, but with a gap" competing reports the critique screens dissect; his reports improve all year; door 17: his method enters the Sijill beside the class's — rival becomes ally. |
| The fog with a face (LOTR: the force behind the quest) | **The Muddler** | (defined in `01` §5) — stain at door 1, named at door 5, faced at door 13, wisp at door 17. |
| The guild quest-givers (LOTR: the households and guilds who set the errands) | **The quest specialists** below | One per door-cluster; each gives the quest, tests the class, and hands over a skill they pass on. |

**Cast development rule:** a specialist appears in their home door's *cold open*
(names the quest), one *mid-door beat* (tests the class), and the *climax or
handoff* (receives the artifact). Specialists never appear outside their door and
cameo rights. Rashed and Zafir are the exceptions by design: Rashed at every
critique screen, Zafir at every act gate.

## 2 · The four pillars (recurring)

### Hafizah — the Lantern Keeper  `hafizah`
- **Who:** an older woman, keeper of the school's little archive where the brass
  lantern lives. Grey headscarf, reading glasses, a cardigan.
- **Function:** frame keeper / mentor. Door 1 cold open: she hands the class the
  lantern and the water-damaged folios — "the ink was not damaged by water; it was
  *moved*". Door 17: she receives the completed folio and the lantern; the class
  becomes the keepers. The tale's bookends (HP: the legacy handed on).
- **Voice:** slow, precise, amused. She never gives answers — she asks the
  question the class is about to ask.
- **Arc:** from keeper who guards the tale to keeper who trusts it to younger
  hands. Her first line and her last line are the same sentence, completed.
- **Visual:** `#6B5B73` cardigan, `#C9CDD3` headscarf, glasses, prop: **the
  lantern** (she carries the real StoryLantern motif).
- **Where:** 1-1 (cold open), 1-5 (the first seal), 7-5 handoff cameo (Act I
  closes — she reads the restored folios), 17-x (the return).

### Ustadh Zafir — the traveling scholar  `zafir`
- **Who:** a middle-aged man who teaches wherever the caravan road takes him; a
  worn satchel, a brass compass on a chain, ink-stained cuffs.
- **Function:** the quest guide. He arrives at act gates with the next door's
  riddle (Nights: the traveler's riddle); at the door-5 climax he is the one who
  *names* the fog ("we have called him the Muddler for thirty years"); before
  door 13 he warns the class what a polished claim looks like from the inside.
- **Voice:** riddles first, explanations only after the class has tried.
- **Arc:** he has chased the fog across three cities; door 13 is the first time a
  class has stood against him *and won*; door 17 he teaches the Sijill entry
  beside Rashed.
- **Visual:** `#4A6B8A` jacket, `#C9A227` sash, compass, ink-stained cuffs.
- **Where:** 2-8 handoff (Act gate teaser), 5-10 climax (names the Muddler),
  6-6 handoff (Act I gate — the riddle of the reef), 7-5 handoff (handover to
  the architect), 13-x (the warning), 17-x (the Sijill entry).

### Rashed — the Rival's Report  `rashed`
- **Who:** a Grade 4 boy from the rival school across the souq; a satchel, a
  clipboard, a habit of presenting before the class has finished the question.
  Age-appropriate, clearly a child, never a mockery.
- **Function:** the gentle rival. He is the author of every "polished, but with a
  gap" report the critique screens dissect (2-8, 5-10, 6-4, 6-6, 7-3, 8-7, 13-7,
  16-6). The gap is always the *same* gap: a missing middle step — the class's
  whole year's skill, turned against him kindly.
- **Voice:** confident, quick, improves audibly. Door 2: "It is finished." Door 7:
  "It is finished — and here is the step I skipped, for you to find." Door 17:
  his method enters the Sijill beside the class's.
- **Arc:** rival → student of the class's method → co-author of the final Sijill
  entry (HP's rival, made constructive; the constructive provocation of the
  young hobbits made gentle).
- **Visual:** boy proportions (same silhouette family as Omar/Zayd), `#7A5CA8`
  jumper, `#F2938F` satchel strap, prop: **clipboard**.
- **Where:** 2-8 (first report), every critique screen of the built doors,
  13-x (he presents at the market too — the Muddler's claims dress as his
  reports), 17-x (the Sijill entry).

### The Muddler  `muddler`
- (Full definition in `01-characters-and-arcs.md` §5.) Boss art exists (boss-13:
  HP bar, hurt-shake, defeat sound). In the fiction lane he is **never a person**:
  a stain, a fog, a claim that sounds right. His "voice" is always a *claim*,
  never a sentence.

## 5 · Visual spec (engine: `SupportCharacter`)

Flat vector, same silhouette family as `StoryCharacter`, adult proportions
(taller body, 100×112 viewBox), each figure defined by: base palette (jacket +
accent), head treatment (hair or simple headscarf shape), and **one held prop** —
the prop is the character (ledger, seal, ruler, tally, quill, compass, oar,
ladle, key, mask, clipboard, balance, astrolabe, satchel). The Muddler is not a
`SupportCharacter` — he keeps his boss art and, in ribbons, is represented only by
the fog motif (a wisp), never a face.

**Guardrails in code:** the supporting-figure data is fiction-lane only; the
Amanah lane and STEM lane never render supporting figures; labels come from the
payload (fictional names), and no label may collide with a documented-account
name (the foundation check lists the banned historical names).
