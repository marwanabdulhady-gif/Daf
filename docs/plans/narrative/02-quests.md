# The seventeen quests — door dossiers

**Companion to `00-epic-architecture.md` and `01-characters-and-arcs.md`.**
Each dossier is the authoring contract for one unit's story lessons.
**Source of truth for the math scope, standard, IXL codes, screen plan and the
exact artifact string remains `story/story-map.json`** — this document adds the
quest layer (giver, place, minion, mission framing) and must never change math
scope. Mission names are the existing per-lesson `storyBeat` values (already in
the map); "the fog whispers" lines are student-facing trap phrasing drawn from
each lesson's `rail.misconception` (teacher rail). Character door beats live in
`01-characters-and-arcs.md` §1–§2.

**Reading a mission row:** `N-M · mission — objective — the fog whispers (trap)`.
The trap is the door's minion in action (unnamed before door 13, named after).

## Act I · Number, provision and systems — *the calling*

### Quest 1 · The census in the brass lantern (Topic 1 · 4.NBT.A)
**Giver:** the Council of Numeria (the lantern, found in the hall). **Place:** the school hall, then the census table.
**The promise (unit question):** Can the class recover a damaged city census without changing what any digit means?
**Artifact to forge:** the restored census folio and place-value key (the population and services brief, unit PBL).
**The Muddler's presence:** the stain is found here — the torn page, the missing labels. The class has no name for it yet; only *the fog*.
**Climax:** 1-5, the seal of the first folio (two teams defend statements before the council). **Reward:** the first seal; door 1 steps light on the Map of the Doors.

| # | Mission | Objective | The fog whispers |
|---|---|---|---|

**Tale close:** the pavilion stands. Everything the year built leads to one last door — and through it, not out of the tale, but *forward*. *Door seventeen is a gate.*

## Act IV · Transfer, exhibition and legacy — *the return*

### Quest 17 · The grand gate and the letter forward (Topic 17 · Grade 5 bridge)
**Giver:** the Council (the commission is the class's own). **Place:** the exhibition hall, the Grand Gate.
**Promise:** Can the class combine the year's ideas to build, test and hand on a trustworthy model?
**Artifact:** the Grand Gate model (with its volume) and **the letter forward** to Grade 5.
**Presence:** **the wisp's face.** The fog returns in new units — decimals to the hundredth, unlike denominators, volume. The class meets it calmly: they have named it, and naming is the first check. The letter forward says so in their own words.
**Climax:** the gate's final test (the small confrontation of the year's last lesson) + the exhibition, where the class presents the whole year's doors. **Reward:** the gate opens; the lantern is handed on; the Map of the Doors is full.

## Appendix · recurring patterns across the quests

- **Every door opens** with the quest-giver stating the promise (unit question) and the
  stakes (the artifact at risk) — the existing tale-open, ≤90s.
- **Every door closes** with the seal on the climax screen, the Map step, and a
  handoff cliffhanger — the existing tale-close, ≤45s.
- **Every door has a named fog** (minion) in its dossier; before door 13 it is
  unnamed ("the fog"), after it the class names what it met.
- **Doors 5, 9, 12 and 17** are *skirmish* doors (small confrontations at their
  climaxes, per the boss template in `00` §7.3); **door 13 is the only great
  confrontation.**
- **The council** is the giver at doors 1, 6, 13 (challenged) and 17; every other
  door has one specialist giver (merchant, foreman, caravan master, architect,
  event manager, clockmaker, diver, docks agent, inspector, keeper) — specialists
  appear in their home door and may return in cameos, never as new dialogue
  before the PR that authors them.
