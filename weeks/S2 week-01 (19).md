## Standards / Skills Checklist

### Fractions
- [ ] Equivalent fractions (models, number lines, × and ÷ by *n/n*)
- [ ] Compare fractions (benchmarks, common denominators)
- [ ] Add and subtract fractions and mixed numbers with like denominators
- [ ] Decompose fractions
- [ ] Multiply a whole number by a fraction; fractions as multiples of unit fractions
- [ ] Elapsed time and fractions of an hour

### Data & decimals
- [ ] Read, make, and interpret line plots with fractional units
- [ ] Relate tenths/hundredths fractions to decimals
- [ ] Compare decimals to hundredths
- [ ] Add 10ths + 100ths; money problems

### Measurement
- [ ] Convert customary length, capacity, weight
- [ ] Convert metric length, capacity, mass
- [ ] Perimeter and area of rectangles; correct units

### Patterns & geometry
- [ ] Generate and analyze number and shape patterns
- [ ] Identify points, lines, rays, angles; classify angles
- [ ] Measure and draw angles; additive angle measures
- [ ] Parallel / perpendicular lines
- [ ] Classify triangles and quadrilaterals
- [ ] Identify and draw lines of symmetry

## Suggested Exam Structure

**Part A — Computation**  
Equivalent fractions, +/− mixed numbers, whole number × fraction, decimal/fraction conversions, unit conversions, perimeter/area.

**Part B — Representations**  
Line plots, number lines, area models, protractor items, pattern tables, classified figures.

**Part C — Problem solving**  
Multi-step fraction and measurement problems, unknown angles, pattern *n*th term, constructed arguments (e.g., “Is every rectangle a rhombus?”).

**Part D — Performance task (optional)**  
One extended task in the style of Volume 2 Topic Performance Tasks.
