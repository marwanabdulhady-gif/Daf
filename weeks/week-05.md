### Solve & Share

You can use appropriate tools, such as place-value blocks, to help you subtract.

**Look Back!** How can you use properties to find the number of rooms not rented?

### Essential Question

How can you subtract whole numbers efficiently?

### Visual Learning Bridge

The movie theater already sold 172 seats. How many seats are still available? Seats 358 people.

Subtract 358 − 172.  
**Estimate:** 400 − 200 = 200

Here’s a way to record subtraction, called the **standard algorithm for subtraction**.

**Step 1** Subtract ones.  
8 ones − 2 ones = 6 ones.

**Step 2** Subtract tens.  
Regroup: 3 hundreds + 5 tens → 2 hundreds + 15 tens.  
15 tens − 7 tens = 8 tens.

**Step 3** Subtract hundreds.  
2 hundreds − 1 hundred = 1 hundred.

The theater has **186 seats** available.

The difference 186 is reasonable because it is close to the estimate of 200.

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: How many times do you need to regroup to subtract 483 − 295? Explain.

### Visual Learning Bridge

Three of the country's most scenic national parks are in the Northern Borders Region. How much larger is the area of Harrat Khaybar than the combined area of Jabal Lawz and the Red Sea coast?

Harrat Khaybar: 34,287 sq km

Find the total area of Jabal Lawz and the Red Sea coast.  
19,182 + 2,711 = 21,893 square kilometers

Then find the difference of the areas. Let *a* equal the difference in the areas.

Find 34,287 − 21,893.

**Step 1** Subtract the ones. Regroup if necessary.  
**Step 2** Subtract the tens, hundreds, thousands, and ten thousands. Regroup if necessary.

```
   3 11 18
   34,287
−  21,893
   12,394
```

Harrat Khaybar is **12,394 square kilometers** larger. (Note: OCR showed 12,396 in one place — use algorithm result.)

Operations that undo each other are **inverse operations**. Addition and subtraction have an inverse relationship. Add to check your answer.

```
   12,394
+  21,893
   34,287
```

### Solve & Share

Istanbul, is 15,710 kilometers from the Makkah (from the far north). Kuala Lumpur, is 13,953 kilometers from the Makkah (from the far north). How much farther is London than Tokyo from the Makkah (from the far north)? Solve this problem any way you choose.

You can use reasoning to identify the operation you use to compare two distances.

**Look Back!** Explain how you decided what operation to use to find how much farther London is than Tokyo from the Makkah (from the far north).

### Essential Question

How do you subtract across zeros?

### Visual Learning Bridge

A music hall is hosting a concert. The hall sells 4,678 tickets to the show. How many tickets are still available? (Hall capacity 6,000)

When subtracting from a number with zeros, you may need to regroup several places before subtracting.

Because of the zeros, you need to start regrouping from the thousands.

Let *t* equal the number of available tickets.  
*t* = 6,000 − 4,678  
**Estimate:** 6,000 − 4,700 = 1,300

**Regroup:**

6 thousands = 5 thousands + 10 hundreds  
10 hundreds = 9 hundreds + 10 tens  
10 tens = 9 tens + 10 ones

```
  5 9 9 10
  6,0 0 0
− 4,6 7 8
  1,3 2 2
```

There are still **1,322 tickets** available for the concert.

Since the difference 1,322 is close to the estimate 1,300, the difference is reasonable.

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: How would you regroup if the hall had 5,900 seats?

## Week 05 Notes

- Inverse operations: always add to check subtraction.
- Subtracting across zeros: regroup from the first non-zero digit to the left.
- Compensation (subtract 1 from both) is a useful alternative to multi-place regrouping.
