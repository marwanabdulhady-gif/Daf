## Math Practices and Problem Solving Handbook

Available at SavvasRealize.com.

**Make Sense of Problems and Persevere in Solving Them**

Good math thinkers make sense of problems and think of ways to solve them. If they get stuck, they don't give up.

### Thinking Habits

- What do I need to find?
- What do I know?
- What's my plan for solving the problem?
- What else can I try if I get stuck?
- How can I check that my solution makes sense?

### Some Ways to Represent Problems

- Draw a Picture
- Make a Bar Diagram
- Make a Table or Graph
- Write an Equation

### Some Math Tools

- Objects
- Grid Paper
- Rulers
- Technology
- Paper and Pencil

## STEM Connection (Topic 1 Preview)

**Kannesteinen Rock in Norway** got its shape from the sea that surrounds it. Water, wind, and ice can change the shapes of rocks over thousands of years. This is called **erosion**.

**enVision STEM Project: Caves**

- Do Research: Find the depths in feet of the 5 deepest caves in the world.
- Journal: Write a Report
  - Make a place-value chart that includes the five depths.
  - Write each depth in expanded form.
  - Use “greater than” or “less than” to compare the depths of two of the caves.
