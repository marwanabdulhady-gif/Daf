### Solve & Share

Find the measure of ∠ABC.

A protractor’s measures sit on a double number line. Which scale should you read?

### Visual Learning Bridge

A **protractor** measures and draws angles. Vertex = common endpoint of the rays.

**To measure ∠PQR**

1. Place the protractor’s **center** on vertex Q.
2. Place one **0° mark** on ray QR.
3. Read where QP crosses the protractor.
4. If the angle is **acute**, use the **lesser** number; if **obtuse**, use the **greater** number.

Example: ∠PQR = **45°**. ∠WTU = **130°**.

**To draw 130°**

Draw ray TU. Center on T, 0° on TU. Majid 130°. Label W. Draw TW.

**Convince Me!** How do you know ∠UTS is 60° and not 120°? *(Acute vs obtuse; which 0° you lined up.)*

### Practice

Measure; classify acute, right, or obtuse. Draw 110° and 50°. Straight line = 180°.

19. Measure all angles at Main St & Pleasant St.
20. Measure an angle, then draw a right angle from one ray; find the other angle.
21. Half a pie shared by Ghazi + 3 friends. Each 25°? *(360°/2 = 180°; 180°/4 = 45°, not 25°.)*
23. **HOT:** Intersecting roads, one angle 35°. Other three measures? (35°, 145°, 35°, 145° — vertical angles equal; adjacent on a line sum to 180°.)
24–25. Measure given angles.

### Solve & Share

Draw ray BC that divides ∠ABD into two smaller angles. Measure each. How is the sum of the two smaller related to the larger?

**Look Back!** Relate with an equation.

### Visual Learning Bridge

Emad draws right ∠ABC, then BE. ∠EBC = 30°. Find ∠ABE without a protractor.

If ∠EBC and ∠ABE do **not overlap**, the measure of ∠ABC equals the **sum** of its parts.

```
n + 30° = 90°
n = 60°
```

**Convince Me!** Straight ∠ABD. ∠DBC = 115°, ∠CBE = 20°. Measure of ∠ABE? Equation.

### Practice
