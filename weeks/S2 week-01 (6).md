### Solve & Share

**Look Back!** Use Structure: How does 4 × ⅓ compare to 4 × ¾? Why?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Dawud lives ¼ mile from school. She walks to and from school each day. How far during a school week? (10 trips)

**One Way — picture**

10 groups of ¼:

```
10 × 1/4 = 10/4 = 2 2/4 = 2 1/2
```

**Another Way — number line**

10 hops of ¼ land at ¹⁰⁄₄ = 2½.

Dawud walks **2½ miles** to and from school each week.

**Convince Me!** Why can both addition and multiplication represent this? Write an equation.

### Another Example

Jassim bikes ⅗ mile each day for 4 days.

Addition: ⅗ + ⅗ + ⅗ + ⅗ = ¹²⁄₅ = 2⅖  
Multiplication: 4 × ⅗ = ¹²⁄₅ = 2⅖

### Solve & Share

Recipe: ⅜ cup orange juice per gallon. How many cups for 8 gallons?

**Look Back!** Be Precise: What units should you use?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Sultan made 2 sundaes, ⅜ pint of ice cream each. How much ice cream?

You can use **structure** when multiplying a fraction and a whole number.

**One Way — unit fraction**

```
2 × 3/4 = 2 × (3 × 1/4) = (2 × 3) × 1/4 = 6 × 1/4 = 6/4 = 1 2/4
```

**Associative Property** of Multiplication.

**Another Way — multiply whole number and numerator**

```
2 × 3/4 = (2 × 3)/4 = 6/4 = 1 2/4
```

Sultan used 1½ (or ⁶⁄₄) pints.

**Convince Me!** Use properties to calculate 3 × ⅖.

21. **Vocabulary:** When you multiply a fraction by a whole number, the **denominator** in the product is the same as the denominator of the fraction. The **numerator** in the product is the product of the **whole number** and the numerator of the fraction.

### Solve & Share

Big Sur Marathon. Sultan’s mother was women’s overall winner (2 h 50 min). Women’s 65–69 winner: 3 h 58 min. How much faster was Sultan’s mother?

**Look Back!** Men 70–74: 4⅓ hours; Sultan’s grandfather (68): 3¾ hours. Difference?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Karim trains ¾ hour per day for 8 days. How many hours? She spends equal time sprinting, walking, jogging. Minutes on each activity?

```
8 × 3/4 = 24/4 = 6 hours
6 × 60 = 360 minutes
360 ÷ 3 = 120 minutes = 2 hours on each activity
```

**Convince Me!** Why multiply to convert 6 hours to minutes?

### Another Example

**Adding time:** 2 h 32 min + 3 h 40 min = 5 h 72 min = **6 h 12 min** (regroup 60 min = 1 h).

**Subtracting time:** 5 h 8 min − 2 h 32 min. Regroup 1 h as 60 min: 4 h 68 min − 2 h 32 min = **2 h 36 min**.

### Units of Time

| 1 hour = 60 minutes | 1 day = 24 hours |
| 1 year = 12 months | 1 week = 7 days |
