### Essential Question

How can you use the Distributive Property to multiply two 2-digit numbers?

### Visual Learning Bridge

There are 25 teams in the Strike Out Club. There are 15 players on each football team. How many players are on all of the teams?

You can use an area model to show the **Distributive Property**.

**One Way — Break apart 15 into 10 + 5.**

```
25 × (10 + 5) = (25 × 10) + (25 × 5)
              = 250 + 125
              = 375
```

**Another Way — Break apart 25 into 20 + 5.**

```
(20 + 5) × 15 = (20 × 15) + (5 × 15)
              = 300 + 75
              = 375
```

**Another Way — Break apart both factors.**

```
        10          5
    ┌──────────┬─────┐
 20 │ 20 × 10  │20×5 │
    │   200    │ 100 │
    ├──────────┼─────┤
  5 │  5 × 10  │ 5×5 │
    │    50    │  25 │
    └──────────┴─────┘
```

200 + 100 + 50 + 25 = **375**

There are **375 players** on the teams.

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: How does an area model illustrate the Distributive Property?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Mr. Diaz ordered a supply of 1,800 pastels. He wants to divide them equally among his class and 5 other art classes. How many pastels does each class receive?

You can use **basic division facts and place value** to divide.

**Find 1,800 ÷ 6.**

The basic division fact is 18 ÷ 6 = 3.  
18 hundreds ÷ 6 = 3 hundreds or 300.  
1,800 ÷ 6 = **300**

Each class will receive **300 pastels**.

If Mr. Diaz stores the pastels so each class will receive new pastels 5 times a year, how many pastels are handed out each of the 5 times?

**Find 300 ÷ 5.**

The basic division fact is 30 ÷ 5 = 6.  
30 tens ÷ 5 = 6 tens or 60.  
300 ÷ 5 = **60**

Each class will receive **60 pastels** 5 times a year.

Division is used to find equal groups.  
**Dividend ÷ Divisor = Quotient**

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: Write the missing dividends for equations such as __ ÷ 7 = 70, __ ÷ 8 = 50, __ ÷ 4 = 800. How did you determine each dividend?

**Look Back!** Is an exact answer or an estimate needed for the problem above? Explain.

### Essential Question

How can you estimate quotients?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Majid wants to make 9 rubber band balls using about the same number of rubber bands for each ball. He bought a jar of 700 rubber bands. Estimate to find about how many rubber bands Majid can use for each ball.

There is more than one way to estimate a quotient. Majid does not need to know the exact number. An estimate is all that is needed.

**Use Compatible Numbers**

Estimate 700 ÷ 9.  
What number close to 700 is easily divided by 9?  
Try multiples of ten near 700.  
710 is not easily divided by 9.  
720 is 72 tens and can be divided by 9.  
720 ÷ 9 = 80  
Majid can use about **80 rubber bands** for each ball.

**Use Multiplication**

Estimate 700 ÷ 9.  
9 times what number is about 700?  
9 × 8 = 72, so 9 × 80 = 720.  
700 ÷ 9 is about **80**.

**Convince Me!** Construct Arguments: What compatible numbers can you use to estimate 132 ÷ 6? Why is rounding not a good way to estimate 132 ÷ 6?
