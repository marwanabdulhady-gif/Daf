**School:** Dar Al Fikr (DAF) Boys School, Jeddah  
**Program:** Grade 4 enVision Mathematics · 2026–2027  
**Aligned with:** DAF Vision & Mission · Saudi Vision 2030 · Islamic identity · Saudi Riyal (SAR)

## 3. Islamic identity (the heart)

Use with **respect**. Do not trivialize worship. Mathematics serves understanding of Allah’s creation and honest dealing.

**Approved contexts**
- Five daily prayers → elapsed time, angles of a clock
- Ramadan → fasting hours, iftar sharing, dates (tamr)
- Zakat and sadaqah → comparison, multi-step giving (honesty, not a fiqh lesson)
- Hajj / Umrah → large numbers, distance, time
- Quran: 30 ajzā’ → factors, division, fractions of a whole
- Masjid geometry → lines, symmetry, Islamic patterns (8-point star, arabesque)
- Stewardship (*khilāfah*) of land, water, and living things
- Precision in measure — a trust (*amānah*); the Prophet ﷺ encouraged exact measure in buying and selling

**Avoid**
- Pork, alcohol, interest (*ribā*) as positive business models
- Halloween, Christmas, Thanksgiving as school celebrations
- Mixing boys and girls as story protagonists in school settings
- Treating sacred rites as mere “fun word problems” without dignity

## 4. Currency — Saudi Riyal (SAR)

- Replace **dollars / $** with **Saudi riyals / SAR**.
- Write amounts as **SAR 35** or **35 riyals**.
- Coins and notes: 1, 5, 10, 50, 100, 500 riyal notes; 1-riyal and 2-riyal coins (halalah = 1/100 riyal, like hundredths in Topic 12).
- **1 riyal = 100 halalah** — this is the natural money–decimal link for Topic 12.
- Computation numbers are usually **kept the same** as the original program so answer keys and partial-product structure still work. Prices are “Grade 4 model amounts,” not market quotes.
