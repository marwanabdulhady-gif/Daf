### Solve & Share

Jawad jogged 75 yards to school. How many feet?

3 feet = 1 yard. Be sure to calculate correctly and use the right units.

**Look Back!** Relationship between number of yards and number of feet?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Majid has a tree swing. Branch 10 ft from ground; swing 2½ ft from ground. How many inches is each rope?

**Customary Units of Length**

| 1 foot (ft) | = 12 inches (in.) |
| 1 yard (yd) | = 3 ft = 36 in. |
| 1 mile (mi) | = 1,760 yd = 5,280 ft |

1 foot is **12 times as long** as 1 inch.

**Step 1:** Rope length in feet: 10 − 2½ = 7½ ft.  
**Step 2:** Convert to inches. 7 × 12 = 84; ½ × 12 = 6; **90 inches** (dump had 7½ → 84+9 if ¾; use 7½ = 90 in.).

From dump: 10 = 9½, minus 2½ = 7½; 7×12=84; ½×12=6 → actually 90. The OCR showed ¾ in one place. I'll use the algorithm: larger → smaller: **multiply**.

**Convince Me!** How do you know the answer is reasonable when converting a larger unit to a smaller unit? *(You get a greater number of smaller units.)*

### Another Example

Majid moved ⅔ yard; Daisy 3⅓ feet. Who moved more? 1 yd = 3 ft so ⅔ yd = 2 ft; Daisy 3⅓ − 2 = 1⅓ ft more.

### Solve & Share

Karim has ½ gallon of juice. How many 1-pint containers can he fill?

**Look Back!** Same process as converting larger length to smaller length?

### Visual Learning Bridge

**Capacity** is how much liquid a container can hold.

```
1 Gallon = 4 quarts = 8 pints = 16 cups
1 Quart  = 2 pints  = 4 cups
1 Pint   = 2 cups
1 Cup    = 8 fluid ounces
```

1 gallon is **4 times as much** as 1 quart.

Ms. Nealy’s class needs 5 gallons of punch. Recipe: 5 pt apple juice, 4 pt soda, 1 pt frozen OJ per batch.

Convert 5 gal → pints: 5 × 8 = **40 pints**.  
Pints in one recipe: 5+4+1=10. Batches: 40 ÷ 10 = 4.  
4 batches: 20 pt apple, 16 pt soda, 4 pt OJ.

**Convince Me!** One gallon equals ■ quarts, ■ pints, or ■ cups.

### Practice

2 c = ■ fl oz; ½ gal = ■ pt; 5 pt = ■ c; 7 qt = ■ c; 12 gal = ■ qt; ½ gal = ■ pt.

Compare: 2 pt ☐ 2 gal; 5 qt ☐ 8 pt; 10 c ☐ 2 qt.
