## Revision days

### Day 1 — Topic 8: Fraction Equivalence and Ordering

**Must-know**

- Equivalent fractions: area models, number lines, × or ÷ numerator and denominator by the same n.
- Benchmarks 0, 1/2, 1.
- Compare with common denominators or same numerators.

**Quick Practice** (4 items, as printed)

1. Show 2/3 = 8/12 three ways (model, number line, multiplication).
1. Simplify 18/24.
1. Compare 5/8 and 7/12.
1. Order: 2/5, 1/2, 3/8.

### Day 2 — Topic 9: Add and Subtract Fractions

**Must-know**

- Like denominators: add/subtract numerators.
- Decompose fractions as sums of unit fractions.
- Mixed numbers: regroup (1 = d/d).

**Quick Practice** (4 items, as printed)

1. 3/8 + 2/8 and 7/8 − 3/8
1. Decompose 5/6 two ways
1. 2 3/5 + 1 4/5
1. 5 1/8 − 2 5/8

### Day 3 — Topic 10: Multiply Fractions × Whole Numbers; Time

**Must-know**

- a/b = a × (1/b)
- n × (a/b) = (n × a)/b
- Elapsed time; fractions of an hour (1/4 h = 15 min)

**Quick Practice** (3 items, as printed)

1. Write 7/8 as a multiple of a unit fraction.
1. 4 × 3/5
1. A 1 3/4-hour show starts at 3:20. When does it end?

### Day 4 — Topic 11: Line Plots

**Must-know**

- Read and make line plots with 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 data.
- Solve put-together / take-apart problems from the plot.

### Day 5 — Topic 12: Decimals and Money

**Must-know**

- Tenths and hundredths as fractions and decimals.
- Compare decimals (line up points; 0.4 = 0.40).
- Add 10ths + 100ths: rewrite 3/10 = 30/100.
- Money word problems.

**Quick Practice** (4 items, as printed)

1. 0.09 as a fraction; 7/10 as a decimal
1. 0.6 ☐ 0.58
1. 3/10 + 27/100
1. 3 items at SAR 1.25; change from SAR 5
