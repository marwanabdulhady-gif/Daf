## More from the week file

### Topic 16 Close

- Add multi-digit (clue-match sums).
- Vocabulary Review: acute/equilateral/isosceles/obtuse/right/scalene triangle; intersecting/parallel/perpendicular lines; line of symmetry; line symmetric; parallelogram; rectangle; rhombus; square; trapezoid.
- True/false and always/sometimes/never:
- An acute triangle has three acute angles (not just one).
- Parallel lines never intersect.
- A rectangle is sometimes a square.
- A trapezoid (this program) does not have two pairs of parallel sides.

- Reteaching Sets A-F: lines; triangles; quadrilaterals; counting lines of symmetry; completing symmetric designs; critique with a counterexample.
- (p. 613) and Performance Task: Hejazi geometric art (p. 615) - parallel lines in a figure; classify 4 congruent triangles; critique "the 4 triangles are inside a square" (other names); scarf quadrilateral names; triangle WXY acute?; decorative plate lines of symmetry.
### Week 16 Notes

- Fold test: matching parts, not just equal area.
- Square: 4 lines; rectangle (not square): 2; rhombus (not square): 2; general parallelogram: 0; circle: infinitely many.
- One counterexample disproves a "all" statement.
- Hierarchy language: "All squares are rectangles" is true; "all rectangles are squares" is not.
