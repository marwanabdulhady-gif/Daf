- enVision STEM Project: Senses and Symmetry
- Chameleons can move their eyes one at a time and see in two directions.
- Why some animals have eyes on the sides vs the front of the head.
- Most animals are the same on both sides. Use a line of symmetry to draw your favorite animal's face. Explain how you know both sides match.

### Week 15 Notes

- Two distinct lines meet at 0 or 1 point - never 2.
- Triangle angle sum 180° ⇒ at most one right or obtuse angle.
- Square ⊂ rectangle ⊂ parallelogram ⊂ quadrilateral; square ⊂ rhombus ⊂ parallelogram. Trapezoid (this program) is not a parallelogram.
