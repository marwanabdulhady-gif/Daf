## More from the week file

### Topic 12 Opening (p. 441)

- Understand and Compare Decimals
- How can you write a fraction as a decimal? How can you locate points on a number line? How do you compare decimals?
- - football and Vision 2030 sport
- A football match can be thought of in 10 equal parts of time. A team "wins" 6 of 10 parts of possession. Write 6/10, an equivalent hundredths fraction, and the decimal. 1 riyal = 100 halalah is the same structure.
- Research a Saudi Pro League or school football moment: how is energy transferred when the ball is kicked? Honesty in recording stats is amānah.
- - hundredth, tenth, place value; shaded grids as tenths/hundredths; compare fractions.
### Week 08 Notes

- 0.6 = 0.60 = 6/10 = 60/100 - equivalence across representations.
- Place-value: tenths are 10 times hundredths (Topic 1 relationship again).
- Critique line plots: frequency (height of stack) vs value (position on the line) vs total (sum of all).
