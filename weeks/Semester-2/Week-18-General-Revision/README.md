## Revision days

### Day 1 — Topic 13: Measurement Equivalence; Perimeter & Area

**Must-know**

- Customary length: 12 in. = 1 ft; 3 ft = 1 yd; 5,280 ft = 1 mi
- Capacity: 8 fl oz = 1 c; 2 c = 1 pt; 2 pt = 1 qt; 4 qt = 1 gal
- Weight: 16 oz = 1 lb; 2,000 lb = 1 T
- Metric: ×10 / ×100 / ×1,000 (m, cm, mm, km; L, mL; kg, g)
- Perimeter = distance around; Area of rectangle = ℓ × w

**Quick Practice** (4 items, as printed)

1. 4 yd = ■ ft = ■ in.
1. 3 gal = ■ qt = ■ c
1. 5 kg = ■ g
1. Rectangle 24 ft by 18 ft: P and A

### Day 2 — Topic 14: Patterns

**Must-know**

- Generate a sequence from a rule; describe extra features (even/odd, multiples).
- Repeating shape patterns: find the nth term using remainder when dividing by the core length.

**Quick Practice** (3 items, as printed)

1. Start at 5, add 8. First 8 terms. Ones-digit pattern?
1. Core ▲■●. 20th shape?
1. Start at 3, multiply by 2. Is 48 in the sequence?

### Day 3 — Topic 15: Angles

**Must-know**

- Point, line, segment, ray, angle, vertex
- Acute / right / obtuse / straight; 1° = 1/360 of a circle
- Measure and draw with a protractor
- Angle measures add; straight line = 180°; around a point = 360°

**Quick Practice** (3 items, as printed)

1. Classify four given angles by type; estimate then measure.
1. Draw 70° and 155°.
1. Two angles on a straight line; one is 118°. Other?

### Day 4 — Topic 16: Lines, Triangles, Quadrilaterals, Symmetry

**Must-know**

- Parallel, intersecting, perpendicular
- Triangles by sides and by angles
- Quadrilateral hierarchy (square ⊂ rectangle ⊂ parallelogram, etc.)
- Lines of symmetry; complete a reflected figure

**Quick Practice** (4 items, as printed)

1. Name all the categories a square belongs to.
1. Sketch an obtuse scalene triangle.
1. Lines of symmetry of a regular hexagon? A non-square rectangle?
1. Critique: "Every parallelogram has line symmetry."
