# How We Embed This in Every Lesson  
**Dar Al Fikr Boys School · Grade 4 Math · 2026–2027**

This file is the **adab layer** (niyyah, sabr, amanah, sitr, muhasabah).

**Lesson architecture is the FIKR Model** (seven stages). See `FIKR_LESSON_FRAME.md` and the FIKR map under every lesson in `week-XX.md`.

Do not run two clocks. FIKR is the skeleton; this file is how we treat the boys inside it.

---

## 1. What “routine” means here

A 9–10-year-old boy at DAF does not need a new speech every day. He needs the **same five moves**, every period, until they become *‘adah* (habit).

| Move | Islamic root | Math job |
|------|----------------|----------|
| 1. Open with **niyyah** | al-Ghazali, al-Zarnuji | *Iqra* box in the week file |
| 2. Honor knowledge (**adab**) | al-Zarnuji — *ta‘zim al-‘ilm* | Listen, raise hands, tools ready |
| 3. Struggle with **sabr** | growth of *ikhtiar / istiqamah* | Solve & Share, mistakes as steps |
| 4. Check with **amanah** | *sidq* — Allah sees what the teacher does not | Self-/peer-mark honestly |
| 5. Close with **muhasabah** | *Ayyuha al-Walad* | 60 seconds: Did I show sabr? Was I honest? Did I help? |

**آداب صفنا** (posted, co-created Week 01):

1. We honor knowledge and each other.  
2. We start with intention.  
3. We are patient with mistakes.  
4. We are honest.  
5. We help, not harm.

Teacher **qudwah**: calm voice, honest marking, never public shame (*sitr*).

## 2. The 45–50 minute lesson (use this every day)

Times are a default. Stretch Visual Learning on a new concept; shorten Independent Practice on a fluency day.

```
0:00–0:02   ARRIVAL + ADAB
            Tools out. Silent greet. Proximity (Tier 0).

0:02–0:04   NIYYAH  (the Iqra box — do not skip)
            “Bismillah. We learn to understand what Allah created,
             to be precise (amanah), and to serve our people
             and Vision 2030.”
            One boy may say it. Class ameen. 20 seconds is enough.

0:04–0:12   SOLVE & SHARE  (from the week file)
            Boys attempt the problem any way they choose.
            Teacher circulates: name sabr (“you tried a second way”).
            Call 2 strategies. Honor the thinking, not only the answer.
            Look Back question = math talk with adab (raise hands).

0:12–0:22   VISUAL LEARNING BRIDGE
            You teach. They copy the model (area, bar, partial products…).
            Convince Me! = short partner hiwar: “Explain why.”
            Ta‘awun: explain, do not just give the answer.

0:22–0:28   GUIDED PRACTICE  (Do You Understand / Do You Know How?)
            You do 1. We do 1. You do 1.
            Beautiful mistake (anonymized) on the board if one appears.

0:28–0:40   INDEPENDENT / PROBLEM SOLVING
            Localized word problems (SAR, Jeddah, Hajj, Green Initiative).
            Honesty protocol before any self-check (see §4).
            Teacher: Tiers 0–2 only unless a pattern of disruption.

0:40–0:45   MUHASABAH + EXIT
            3 fists-to-chest or mini whiteboard:
              Sabr?  Amanah?  Ta‘awun?
            One sentence in the math journal (English; Arabic value-word allowed).
            Dismiss with salam.
```

**If the period is 40 minutes:** cut Independent by 5 minutes; never cut Niyyah or Muhasabah.

**If it is a 3-Act / Performance Task day:** Solve & Share becomes the Act; Visual Learning is the model after the reveal; Independent is the write-up.

## 3. How to use a `week-XX.md` file (teacher prep — 10 minutes)

Do this **the afternoon before**, not during the bell.

1. Open `weeks/semester#/week-XX.md`.  
2. Circle **today’s lesson** only (e.g. Lesson 2-3). Ignore the rest of the week.  
3. Highlight:
   - Solve & Share  
   - one Visual Learning example you will write on the board  
   - 4 Independent items (not all of them)  
   - the *Iqra · Identity · 2030* line you will say (same words all week is better than a new speech)  
4. Write **one** localized hook if the file’s story needs a 10-second Jeddah/DAF bridge  
   (“Umm Fahad’s SAR 100 notes” → “like the notes in your father’s wallet”).  
5. Prepare tools (grid paper, fraction strips, protractor) — *appropriate tools* is itself adab toward the work.  
6. Decide the **honesty moment**: which 4 items will boys self-mark?

You do **not** read the whole week file to the class. The file is the teacher’s script. The boys see the board, the book, and the five آداب.

## 4. Honesty protocol (amanah) — same words every time

Before they mark their own or a partner’s work:

> “Correcting this honestly is **amanah**. Allah sees what I do not see. A wrong mark that you fix is *istidrak*. A wrong mark that you hide is not.”

Then:

- Answer key on the board or under a flap.  
- Boys mark in a **different color**.  
- You spot-check 4–5 books at random — framed as fairness, not suspicion.  
- A mismatch = **maw‘izah** (quiet teaching), never accusation the first time.

This is the math version of *sidq*. It is itself “graded” on the affective indicator (0–2), not as a punishment.

## 5. Behavior in the same 45 minutes (do not run a second system)

**Tier 0 — Prevention**  
Engaging Solve & Share, clear routine, you moving. Most problems die here.

**Tier 1 — Indirect**  
“Some of us are ready for the next step when tools are down.” No names. Prophetic *sitr*.

**Tier 2 — Quiet word at the desk**  
“I know you can show adab here. Hands still; we honor the one speaking.” 10 seconds.

**Tier 3 — Private reflection card** (after class or in a 2-minute pause, not as a show)  
What happened? Which of the five آداب slipped? One repair (redo, apologize, reset the group). Logged.

**Tier 4 — Parent as shared amanah**  
Only after a prior **positive** note home. Meeting = plan, not complaint.

**Targhib (every period, 3–5 times):**  
Name the **value**, not “good boy.”  
“That was *sabr* — third attempt.”  
“That was *ta‘awun* — you explained the bar diagram.”  
Class jar: 20 pebbles → 10 minutes of a math game Friday. Collective, not a prize shop.

**Beautiful mistakes wall (one card a week):**  
Anonymous wrong method that taught the class. Caption: *sabr → ihsan*.

## 6. The week as a rhythm (not 5 identical days)

| Day | Math emphasis (from the week file) | Tarbiyah emphasis |
|-----|------------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Sun** | New lesson 1 — Visual Learning heavy | Re-state آداب; niyyah is slow and clear |
| **Mon** | New lesson 2 | Sabr on the hard model; beautiful mistake if it appears |
| **Tue** | New lesson 3 / Problem Solving | Ta‘awun: partner bar diagrams |
| **Wed** | Guided + Independent mixed | Amanah self-check (full protocol) |
| **Thu** | Fluency path / vocabulary / exit ticket | Muhasabah written; one praise note home (character, not only marks) |

**Week 01 of the year:** co-create آداب صفنا; teach each value with a 3-minute story/hadith; practice the 45-minute skeleton on diagnostic items. Do not rush Topic 1 until the skeleton holds.

**Weeks 17–18 (revision) and 19 (exams):** same open/close. Shorter Solve & Share. Honesty protocol on every paper.

## 7. Where Vision 2030 and Islamic identity actually sit

They are **not** a Saturday assembly. They sit in three places you already have:

1. **Niyyah (2 min)** — the Iqra box. Same sentence all year.  
2. **Word problems** — already localized (SAR, Jeddah, Haramain, Green Initiative, Hajj counts, masjid geometry). Teach them as math. One extra sentence of meaning is enough: “Precision in measure is a trust.”  
3. **STEM / Pick a Project (once per topic)** — journal + research. This is Nama’s *mahari* (skill) + *wijdani* (affect) without adding a sixth subject.

Do **not** add a 15-minute “values lecture” inside the math hour. That breaks tadarruj and eats Visual Learning. Qudwah + 20 seconds of niyyah + named praise **is** the tarbiyah.

## 8. Tracking (keep it light enough to last)

Five scores, 0–2 except niyyah 0–1, **per boy per day**. Tick during independent work; do not stop the lesson to tally.

| Value | What you saw today |
|-------|--------------------|
| Adab | Hands / listening |
| Sabr | Re-attempt without acting out |
| Amanah | Honest mark or admitted error |
| Ta‘awun | Explained, not just answered |
| Niyyah / muhasabah | Opened and closed with the class |

Weekly rollup: one highlight per boy (send **praise** home first). Flags: strong ta‘awun + low sabr → sit him with a calmer partner and easier first item tomorrow (*tadarruj*), not a lecture.

If the class is hitting Tier 3 often, **change the task**, not the child (Ibn Khaldun).

## 9. First 15 school days — install the habit

| Day | Install |
|-----|---------|
| 1 | آداب صفنا poster. Niyyah script. Diagnostic (Week 01 file). |
| 2 | Muhasabah 60 seconds. Teacher models a “beautiful mistake.” |
| 3 | Solve & Share protocol: 2 strategies, honor both. |
| 4 | Partner ta‘awun rule: “explain, don’t tell.” |
| 5 | Honesty protocol on 4 items. |
| 6–10 | Full 45-minute skeleton on Lessons 1.1–1.3. Tiers 0–2 only. |
| 11–15 | First class-jar goal completed. First character notes home. Honesty protocol is normal. |

Benchmark: by Day 15, boys can name the five آداب and the sentence “a mistake is a step to understanding.” If they cannot, stay here — do not add Topic 2.

## 11. What this is not

- Not a second curriculum.  
- Not PBIS with Arabic labels. Each move traces to adab, niyyah, sitr, rifq, islah, muhasabah (see the tarbiyah source table).  
- Not extra homework about values. The week file **is** the work.  
- Not public points on the board that shame. Class jar = together. Individual scores = teacher clipboard.
