### Essential Question

### Visual Learning Bridge

A total of 277 people signed up to audition for a talent show. Five people at a time were brought in for a group interview. How many group interviews were needed to audition all 277 people?

Find 277 ÷ 5.

Divide until the remainder is less than the divisor!

**Estimate:** How many 5s are in 277?  
40 × 5 = 200  
50 × 5 = 250  
60 × 5 = 300  ← 60 is too many.  
There are at least fifty 5s in 277.

```
        50
         5
      ------
  5 ) 277
     −250
       27
      −25
        2
```

Partial quotients: 50 + 5 = 55  
277 ÷ 5 = **55 R2**

There were 55 group interviews with 5 people in each group. Two people remain. A total of **56 group interviews** were needed to audition all 277 people.

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: How can you use multiplication and addition to check the answer above?  
(55 × 5) + 2 = 275 + 2 = 277 ✓

### Essential Question

### Visual Learning Bridge

Majid said the Rangers scored 3 times as many runs as the Stars. Jody said the Rangers scored 8 more runs than the Stars. Could both Majid and Jody be correct? (Stars scored 4 runs.)

**Compare with Multiplication** — Find 3 times as many as 4 runs.

```
Stars' runs:     [ 4 ]
Rangers' total:  [ 4 ][ 4 ][ 4 ]
```

Let *m* = the runs scored by the Rangers according to Majid.  
*m* = 3 × 4  
*m* = **12 runs**

**Compare with Addition** — Find 8 more than 4 runs.

```
Stars' runs:     [ 4 ]
Rangers' total:  [ 4 ][ 8 more ]
```

Let *j* = the runs scored by the Rangers according to Jody.  
*j* = 4 + 8  
*j* = **12 runs**

According to both Majid and Jody, the Rangers scored 12 runs. **Both could be correct.**

**Convince Me!** Construct Arguments: Describe when you would use multiplication and when you would use addition to make a comparison.

### Essential Question

### Visual Learning Bridge

Khalid and Salman are twins that attend different colleges. Khalid's college is four times as far from home as Salman's college. Khalid travels 192 miles to college. How far does Salman travel to college?

Multiplication and division have an **inverse relationship**.

Write a multiplication equation to find the number of miles Salman travels to college.

The number of miles Khalid travels, or 192 miles, is 4 times as far as Salman travels.

What number times 4 equals 192?

Let *m* = the miles Salman travels to college.

```
Khalid:   [ m ][ m ][ m ][ m ]  = 192 miles
Salman:  [ m ]
```

If 192 = 4 × *m*, then *m* = 192 ÷ 4.  
*m* = **48 miles**

Salman travels **48 miles** to college.

```
  4 ) 192
     −160    (40 × 4)
       32
      −32    (8 × 4)
        0
```

**Convince Me!** Use Structure: When do you use division to make a comparison?
