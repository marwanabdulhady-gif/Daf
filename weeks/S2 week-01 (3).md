### Solve & Share

Kareem and Jassim painted ⅓ of a sports banner green and ⅙ purple. How much of the banner have they painted?

**Look Back!** Kareem says ⅛ + ⅛ = ²⁄₈. Jassim says ⅛ + ⅛ + ⅛ = ⅜. Use a tool to decide who is correct.

### Visual Learning Bridge

Ten canoeing teams: five silver canoes, two brown. What fraction are either silver or brown?

You can use tools such as **fraction strips** to add two or more fractions.

**Find ⁵⁄₁₀ + ²⁄₁₀.** Five strips joined with two strips are seven strips.

Add the numerators. Write the sum over the **like denominator**.

```
5/10 + 2/10 = 7/10
```

⁷⁄₁₀ of the canoes are either silver or brown.

Number line: join five tenths-segments and two tenths-segments.

**Convince Me!** What two fractions would you add to find green or brown canoes? What is the sum?

### Guided / Independent Practice

Find sums with like denominators using tools: ⅓ + ⅓; ²⁄₅ + ²⁄₅; ⁵⁄₁₂ + ²⁄₁₂; ³⁄₈ + ²⁄₈; etc.

### Solve & Share

Karim has ⅚ pounds of chili for three bowls. Amounts need not be equal. How much could go in each bowl?

**Decompose** means to break into parts. **Compose** means to combine parts.

### Visual Learning Bridge

Khalid wants to leave ⅙ of her garden empty. What are some ways she can plant the rest (⅚)?

**One Way:** four ⅙ sections of blue flowers and one ⅙ of red peppers.  
⅚ = ⁴⁄₆ + ⅙.

**Another Way:** ⅙ green beans + ⅙ yellow squash + ⅙ red peppers + two ⅙ blue flowers.  
⅚ = ⅙ + ⅙ + ⅙ + ²⁄₆.

**Convince Me!** Show why ⅚ = ³⁄₆ + ²⁄₆ and ⅚ = ⅙ + ²⁄₆ + ²⁄₆.

### Another Example — Mixed numbers

A **mixed number** has a whole-number part and a fraction part.

Decompose 3⅛: 1 + 1 + 1 + ⅛, or ⁸⁄₈ + ⁸⁄₈ + ⁸⁄₈ + ⅛ = ²⁵⁄₈.

### Practice

Decompose each fraction or mixed number in two different ways: ⅘, ⁷⁄₈, 1⅔, 2¼, 4, 1⅙, …

### Visual Learning Bridge

Pets Club survey. What fraction chose a hamster or a dog?

Add the numerators. Write the sum over the like denominator.

```
2/12 + 4/12 = 6/12 = 1/2
```

One half of the club members chose a hamster or a dog.

## Week 03 Notes

- Decompose in many ways — unit fractions or mixed chunks.
- Sums ≥ 1 → mixed numbers.
