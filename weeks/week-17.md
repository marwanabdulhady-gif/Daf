## Day 1 — Topic 1: Generalize Place Value Understanding

### Must-know

- Read and write numbers through 1,000,000 (standard form, expanded form, number name).
- A digit in one place has **ten times** the value of the same digit one place to the right.
- Compare whole numbers using place value; record with >, <, =.
- Round to any place using a number line or the “5 or greater” rule.

### Quick Practice

1. Write 408,062 in expanded form and using number names.
2. How many times greater is the 7 in 70,048 than the 7 in 17,992?
3. Compare: 72,013 ☐ 72,103
4. Round 181,376 to the nearest thousand and to the nearest hundred.
5. Construct an argument: Is 437,160 greater than 43,716? Explain using place value.

### Vocabulary check

period · place value · expanded form · rounding · conjecture · millions

## Day 2 — Topic 2: Fluently Add and Subtract Multi-Digit Whole Numbers

### Must-know

- Mental math: make ten, compensation, properties (Commutative, Associative, Identity).
- Estimate sums and differences by rounding; use the estimate to check reasonableness.
- Standard algorithm for addition and subtraction, including **subtracting across zeros**.
- Inverse operations: add to check subtraction.

### Quick Practice

1. Use mental math: 1,985 + 2,595
2. Estimate: 12,642 − (4,298 + 2,149). Is an exact answer needed?
3. Add: 24,595 + 19,255. Check with an estimate.
4. Subtract: 6,000 − 4,678
5. A bar-diagram reasoning problem: toys collected two weeks (3,288 and 1,022); 1,560 donated to one charity. How many to the other?

### Vocabulary check

algorithm · compensation · variable · inverse operations · estimate

## Day 3 — Topic 3: Multiply by 1-Digit Numbers

### Must-know

- Multiply by 10, 100, 1,000 using basic facts and place value / Associative Property.
- Estimate products (rounding or compatible numbers).
- Arrays and **partial products**; **area models** and the **Distributive Property**.
- Mental math: break apart, rearrange, compensate.
- Model with math: bar diagrams and equations for “times as many.”

### Quick Practice

1. 8 × 700 and 8 × 7,000
2. Estimate 9 × 1,993
3. Find 5 × 13 using an array and partial products.
4. Find 8 × 25 using an area model.
5. Mental math: 4 × 325 and 3 × 398
6. 9 teams each judge 13 painters and 14 sculptors. How many artists? Bar diagram + equation.

### Vocabulary check

array · partial product · Distributive Property · numerical expression · Associative / Commutative Property of Multiplication

## Day 4 — Topic 4: Multiply by 2-Digit Numbers

### Must-know

- Multiples of 10: 30 × 80 = 3 tens × 8 tens = 24 hundreds = 2,400.
- Area models / arrays for 2-digit × multiple of 10 and for 2-digit × 2-digit (four partial products).
- Estimate with rounding **or** compatible numbers.
- Distributive Property: 25 × 15 = (20 + 5) × (10 + 5), etc.

### Quick Practice

1. 50 × 60
2. 20 × 24 (area model)
3. Estimate 26 × 12 two ways (rounding and compatible numbers).
4. Find 25 × 15 using an area model (all four partial products).
5. Which is greater: 39 × 21 or 32 × 32? Use estimation first, then decide if you need an exact product.

### Vocabulary check

compatible numbers · factors · product · area model
