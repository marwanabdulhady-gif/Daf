### Solve & Share

Laith’s puppy Badr weighed ½ pound at birth. Weight in ounces?

**Look Back!** How did you know you needed to convert units?

### Visual Learning Bridge

**Weight** is how heavy an object is.

| 1 pound (lb) | = 16 ounces (oz) |
| 1 ton (T) | = 2,000 lb |

1 pound is **16 times as heavy** as 1 ounce.

Majid’s dinner: ⅔ lb pasta, ⅝ lb meatballs, 8 oz sauce. How many 6-oz servings?

Convert pasta and meatballs to ounces (×16), add 8 oz sauce = 24 oz.  
24 ÷ 6 = **4 servings**.

**Convince Me!** How do you convert a larger unit of weight to a smaller unit? *(Multiply.)*

Would tons make sense for Majid’s dinner? No — tons are for very heavy objects.

### Practice

### Solve & Share

Find the length of a marker in cm and mm. Relationship?

**Look Back!** Toby’s giant pencil is 25 cm. Length in mm?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Karim jumped 2 m; Ghazi 175 cm. Who jumped farther?

**Metric Units of Length**

| 1 cm | = 10 mm |
| 1 m | = 100 cm = 1,000 mm |
| 1 km | = 1,000 m |

1 meter is **100 times as long** as 1 halalahimeter.

2 m × 100 = 200 cm. 200 cm > 175 cm. **Karim jumped farther.**

**Convince Me!** Sultan says 5 km = 500 m. Agree? *(No: 5 × 1,000 = 5,000 m.)*

### Another Example

Khalid’s rosebud 2.4 cm; Laith’s 1.8 cm. Linear model. Khalid’s longer.

### Practice

### Solve & Share

Jad has 3 L of water. Milliliters? Mass in grams? (1 L of water has mass 1 kg = 1,000 g.)

Some water bottles hold 1 L = 1,000 mL.

**Look Back!** Why convert units?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Luqman needs 8 L apple juice; has 5,000 mL. Enough?

**Metric Capacity and Mass**

| 1 liter (L) | = 1,000 milliliters (mL) |
| 1 gram (g) | = 1,000 milligrams (mg) |
| 1 kilogram (kg) | = 1,000 g |

1 liter is **1,000 times as great** as 1 milliliter; 1 kg is 1,000 times 1 g.

8 × 1,000 = 8,000 mL. 8,000 > 5,000. Needs **3,000 mL more**.

**Mass** is the amount of matter something contains.

**Convince Me!** Why convert liters to milliliters?

### Another Example

2 kg of apples make 1 L juice. Grams of apples: 2 × 1,000 = 2,000 g.

### Practice

6 g = ■ mg; 9 L = ■ mL. Unit for medicine in a pill (mg); ink in a pen (mL); mass of a pencil (g); your mass (kg); blood in your body (L).

13. 4 zucchini ≈ 2 kg. About how many grams is 1?
14. **HOT:** Sofa 30 kg; pillow 300 g. How many pillows equal the sofa’s mass?
15. Correct comparison. 16. Complete kg/g table.
