### STEM Connection (Topic 1 Preview)

- Kannesteinen Rock in Norway got its shape from the sea that surrounds it. Water, wind, and ice can change the shapes of rocks over thousands of years. This is called erosion.
- enVision STEM Project: Caves
- Do Research: Find the depths in feet of the 5 deepest caves in the world.
- Journal: Write a Report
- Make a place-value chart that includes the five depths.
- Write each depth in expanded form.
- Use "greater than" or "less than" to compare the depths of two of the caves.
