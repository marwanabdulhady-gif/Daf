## More from the week file

### Topic 7 Close

- Add and subtract multi-digit whole numbers (path from START to FINISH).
- Vocabulary Review: array, composite number, factor, factor pairs, generalization, multiple, prime number, whole number.
- Reteaching Sets A-E: arrays for factor pairs; listing factors; repeated reasoning; prime vs composite; multiples. Topic Assessment Practice
- A. How many arrays can Khalid make? List all possible arrays.
- B. How many factors are there for 36? Write them. What do you notice?
- C. Write all the factor pairs for 36. Is 36 prime or composite? Explain.

1. **5.** Select all equations that have a dividend that is a multiple of 4 and a quotient that is a factor of 18.
1. **6.** Write 3 multiples and 3 factors for 24.

### Week 14 Notes

- 1 is neither prime nor composite.
- The only even prime is 2.
- Every multiple of 4 is a multiple of 2; every multiple of 9 is a multiple of 3.
- Factors of n divide n; multiples of n are n, 2n, 3n, ...
