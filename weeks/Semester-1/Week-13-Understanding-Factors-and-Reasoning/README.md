## More from the week file

### Topic 7 Opening

- enVision STEM Project: Analyzing the Animal Kingdom
- Animals have traits that allow them to survive in their habitats. Penguins' dark feathers absorb heat from the sun. Some penguins live in some of the coldest places on Earth. As a defense against the cold, emperor penguins huddle together in large groups.
- Suppose 64 penguins form a huddle to keep warm. Use a grid to draw all the possible arrays for 64.
- If a huddle of 72 penguins breaks apart, how many different ways can the penguins form equal groups? Is 72 prime or composite? Write the factor pairs of 72.

- Pick a Project:
- Model a Campground - Where is the caves of Shada?
- Create a Basketball Arena - How many people can fill a college basketball arena?
- Design a Plant Array for a Store Display - How many arrays of potted plants do you see?

- 3-ACT MATH Preview: Can-Do Attitude
### Week 13 Notes

- Factor pairs come in commutative pairs; stop when a pair repeats.
- Square numbers (16 = 4 × 4) have an odd number of factors.
- Shortcut: you only need to test divisors up to the square root (when pairs start repeating).
