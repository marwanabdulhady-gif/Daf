### Topic 3 Opening

- (Vision 2030 heritage)
- Topographic maps show mountains, wadis, and seas. Jabal Sawda and the Red Sea are part of the land Allah entrusted to us.
- Do Research: Three features on a map of Saudi Arabia. Two facts each.
- Journal: Height or depth. Estimate 10 times those measures. How might this help Vision 2030 tourism (AlUla, Red Sea Project)?
