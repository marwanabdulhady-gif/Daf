## More from the week file

### Topic 4 Opening

- STEM Project: Renewable energy - Saudi Green Initiative (Vision 2030)
- The Kingdom is planting trees and building solar and wind farms so we use what Allah provided without waste.
- Do Research: Solar, wind, and other renewables used or planned in Saudi Arabia (NEOM, Sakaka, Dumat Al-Jandal).
- Journal: Draw an array with 15 rows for turbines in a wind farm. How many turbines?
- A turbine produces 63 megawatt-hours a week. Energy in one year (52 weeks)?
- Duʿā' of stewardship: we do not waste water or power.

- Pick a Project:
- 4A: Explain the Processes - How high would a stack of sabal palms be?
- 4B: Write a Report About the Northern Mockingbird - Can you estimate the weight of dozens of birds?
- 4C: Create an Array Poster - How many soccer players start in the Women's AFC Asian Cup / World Cup?
- 4D: Compare Shot Masses - How much weight can shotputters throw?

- Review What You Know: product, array, variable; multiplication facts and 2-digit × 1-digit; rounding to hundreds and thousands.
### Week 08 Notes

- 10 × 10 = 100 is the key place-value idea for 2-digit × multiple of 10.
- Area models for 20 × 24 show two partial products, not four (because one factor is a multiple of 10).
- Compatible numbers (25 × 10, 50 × 20, etc.) often give a tighter estimate than rounding both factors.
