## More from the week file

### Topic 8 Opening (p. 289)

- Some animals use their senses differently from humans. The entire body of a catfish is covered with taste-sensitive cells. They use their sense of taste to locate food that is far away in the water.
- Do Research: Find how animals use special senses (echolocation, electricity, magnetism). Include where the animal lives and how the sense is used.
- Journal: Some spiders rely on sight. Most spiders have 8 eyes. Draw a spider using some shaded circles as eyes and some empty circles as eyes.
- Write a fraction that names shaded spider eyes to total spider eyes. Write three equivalent fractions.

- (p. 290) Vocabulary: denominator · numerator · fraction · unit fraction Unit fractions: 2 copies of 1/3 is ____; 3 copies of 1/4 is ____; 4 copies of 1/5 is ____.
