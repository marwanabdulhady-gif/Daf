### Solve & Share

A 1982 halalah weighs about 0.11 oz; a 2013 halalah about 0.09 oz. Which weighs more?

Thinking about place value can justify your reasoning.

**Look Back!** Sultan and Dawud each have 0.75 of an orange left. Same amount? Explain. *(Same-size wholes.)*

### Visual Learning Bridge

Dawud 10.11 seconds; Salman 10.09 seconds. Who had the faster time? (Lesser time is faster.)

**Hundredths grids:** 11 hundredths > 9 hundredths.

**Place value:** Start at the left. Whole numbers the same. Tenths: 1 tenth > 0 tenths. So **10.11 > 10.09**. Salman had the faster time.

**Number line:** the number farther right is greater.

**Convince Me!** Write four different digits to make 0._8 < 0._7 and 0._5 > 0._9.

### Another Example

0.23 vs 0.32 with place-value blocks (flat = one whole) or number line. 0.23 < 0.32.

### Practice

Write >, <, or = : 0.70 ☐ 0.57; 0.41 ☐ 0.14; 6.28 ☐ 7.31; 1.1 ☐ 1.10; 0.08 ☐ 0.7; 3.40 ☐ 3.4; SAR 21.50 ☐ SAR 20.99.

Saad: “0.20 > 0.2 because 20 > 2.” **Disagree** — 0.20 = 0.2.

21. Venus fly trap 0.43 s vs 0.6 s — faster time? Place-value blocks.
22. Fishing lures 0.5 oz vs other.
24. **HOT:** Larger bottle 0.81 L, smaller 1.1 L. Can you tell which has more water? *(Yes, compare the decimal amounts; bottle size is extra information about capacity, not contents.)*
25. Quartz 3.76 oz vs garnet 3.68 oz — tool and place value.

### Solve & Share

Mural in 100 equal parts. Majid’s class painted ⁴⁄₁₀; Khalid’s class ⁵⁄₁₀₀. How much have they painted?

**Look Back!** How much remains? As a decimal.

### Visual Learning Bridge

Saad collected ⁴⁄₁₀ of a goal; Jad ⁵⁄₁₀₀. How much of their goal?

Use **like denominators**. Rename ⁴⁄₁₀ as an equivalent fraction with denominator 100:

```
4/10 = (4 × 10)/(10 × 10) = 40/100
40/100 + 5/100 = 45/100
```

**Convince Me!** Why is the denominator of the total 100 and not 200?

### Solve & Share

Flash drive SAR 24 including tax. Customer buys 3, pays SAR 80. How much change?

**Look Back!** Generalize: How can you estimate and check if your solution is reasonable?

### Visual Learning Bridge

Majid buys a toy airplane SAR 3.32 and a toy car SAR 1.12. How much does he spend? How much more does the airplane cost?

**Add:** bills then coins. SAR 4.00 + SAR 0.40 + SAR 0.04 = **SAR 4.44**.

**Subtract:** start with airplane, take away car. Remaining SAR 2.00 + SAR 0.20 = **SAR 2.20** more.

You can draw or use bills and coins. Place value helps add or subtract.

## Week 09 Notes

- Compare decimals like whole numbers: line up the decimal point; first differing place wins.
- 0.4 = 0.40; trailing zeros in tenths/hundredths don’t change value.
- Only unlike-denominator addition in Grade 4: tenths + hundredths via ×10/×10.
- Money is hundredths of a riyal.
