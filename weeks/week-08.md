### Essential Question

How can you multiply by multiples of 10?

### Visual Learning Bridge

The number of visitors of each age group for the Jeddah Season park are shown below. How many children visit the park in 30 days?

**One Way — Basic facts and place value**

```
30 × 80 = 3 tens × 8 tens
        = 24 hundreds
        = 2,400
```

2,400 children visit the park in 30 days.

**Another Way — Break apart numbers. Use the Commutative Property and the Associative Property of Multiplication.**

```
30 × 80 = (3 × 10) × (8 × 10)
        = 3 × 8 × 10 × 10
        = (3 × 8) × (10 × 10)
        = 24 × 100
        = 2,400
```

**Convince Me!** Look for Relationships: Use place value or properties of operations to determine how many adults age 65 and older visit the park in 30 days.

### Another Example

Use the properties of operations to find 50 × 60.

```
50 × 60 = 5 × 10 × 6 × 10
        = (5 × 6) × (10 × 10)
        = 30 × 100
        = 3,000
```

If the product of the basic fact ends in zero, the product has one more zero than you see in the factors.

### Solve & Share

**Look Back!** Construct Arguments: Why did you choose the two factors that you did? How do you know your factors will give the closest estimate of the product?

### Essential Question

What strategies can I use to estimate products of two 2-digit numbers?

### Visual Learning Bridge

The workers picked 14 dozen apples at Ms. Fares's apple grove and 12 dozen apples at Mr. Stuart's apple grove. There are 12 apples in one dozen. About how many apples did the workers pick?

14 + 12 = 26 dozen apples picked in total.

**One Way — Use rounding** to estimate 26 × 12.

Round 26 to the nearest ten → 30.  
Round 12 to the nearest ten → 10.  
30 × 10 = 300  
The workers picked about **300 apples**.

**Another Way — Use compatible numbers** to estimate 26 × 12.

Replace the factors with numbers that are close and easy to multiply.  
26 is close to 25. 12 is close to 10.  
25 × 10 = 250  
The workers picked about **250 apples**.

Your answer can be an estimate because an exact answer is not necessary.

**Convince Me!** Reasoning: Saud said she could find an estimate for 26 × 12 by rounding only 1 factor and multiplying 26 × 10. Do you agree? Explain.

## Week 08 Notes

- 10 × 10 = 100 is the key place-value idea for 2-digit × multiple of 10.
- Area models for 20 × 24 show two partial products, not four (because one factor is a multiple of 10).
- Compatible numbers (25 × 10, 50 × 20, etc.) often give a tighter estimate than rounding both factors.
