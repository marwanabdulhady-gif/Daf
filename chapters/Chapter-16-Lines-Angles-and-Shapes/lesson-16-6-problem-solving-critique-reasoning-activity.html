Stage 5 STEAM Production Builds
Choose 1 of 3 student production artifacts to build and defend against the Proof Critic.
mini-project
The classified symmetric pavilion plan — the unit's own artifact: design a small pavilion elevation with specified line relationships (one square corner, one acute corner, one parallel pair), classify every triangle and quadrilateral it uses, mark every line of symmetry, and present the defended plan with counterexamples tested, signed by its makers.
ask what the pavilion must contain → plan the features and shapes → draw, classify and fold-test every part → test broad labels with counterexamples → present the signed pavilion plan
poster
A claim-vs-counterexample poster: publish four geometry claims, mark each supported or disproved, and draw the decisive evidence (the shared attribute or the one shape that breaks it) beneath each.
ask what would decide each claim → plan the four rows → draw the evidence per claim → test one counterexample per 'false' → share the poster on the wall
presentation AI in the loop
A design-critique hearing with the AI as inspector: present your pavilion, let the AI raise three challenges to your classifications and symmetry claims, answer each with measured evidence (or a counterexample of its labels) yourself on camera.
ask what the inspector will challenge → plan the three defences → request the AI's challenges → test each against the geometry → present the defended pavilion on the wall
AI in the Loop (Critic / Defense Rule)
The AI examines, challenges, and audits candidate calculations. Students verify and build every piece themselves — AI is a critic, never an author.
